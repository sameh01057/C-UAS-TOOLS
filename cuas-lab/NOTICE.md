## Specifications & modelling
Specifications are drawn from manufacturers' **publicly published** materials; a source is
recorded with each entry. Where a figure is not publicly disclosed (e.g. some effector ranges),
it is **flagged as a sim assumption** in the data and shown with a `*` in the UI — it is not a
spec. The behaviour of each component is **modelled from its published specs**, not reproduced
from its hardware or firmware.

## Scope of results
Output from this lab is **representative and faithful in kind**, not a measurement of any real
fielded system or any customer deployment, and not the result of exploiting real RF or C2 links.
Exact magnitudes on a live system can only be determined by an actual security assessment. This
scope statement travels with exported results (CSV) and is shown in the UI.
