## Specifications & modelling
Specifications are drawn from manufacturers' **publicly published** materials; a source is
recorded with each entry. Where a figure is not publicly disclosed, it is **flagged as a sim
assumption** (`range_disclosed: False`, shown with `*` in the UI). The behaviour of each
component is **modelled from its published specs**, not reproduced from its hardware or firmware.

## Scope of results
Output from this lab is **representative and faithful in kind**, not a measurement of any real
fielded robot or any customer deployment, and not the result of exploiting a real robot's buses.
Exact magnitudes on a live system can only be determined by an actual security assessment. This
scope statement travels with exported results (CSV) and is shown in the UI.
